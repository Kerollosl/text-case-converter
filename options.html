Text Case Converter

Select text on any page, then right-click and choose Convert Case, or press a shortcut.

Shortcuts

    Only four cases get shortcuts (Chrome allows four per extension). camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case are in the right-click menu.

    Privacy

    Text Case Converter runs only when you pick a conversion. It touches only your current selection on the current tab, collects nothing, and sends nothing anywhere. Privacy policy · Support
